# Problem Statement

## 1. Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing an e-commerce order management process and identifying opportunities for improvement.

## 2. Business Problem
The online store is experiencing several operational challenges:

- Delays in processing customer orders.
- Difficulty tracking order status.
- Duplicate orders.
- Increased customer complaints due to delivery delays.

## 3. Business Impact
These issues may lead to:

- Lower customer satisfaction.
- Increased operational workload.
- Reduced customer trust.
- Potential loss of sales.

## 4. Project Objective
The objective is to analyze the current order management process, identify business needs, and propose improvements to make order processing more efficient.

## 5. Expected Outcome
A clear set of business requirements and a proposed process that improves order management and customer experience.
