# Stakeholder Analysis

## 1. Overview
This document identifies the key stakeholders involved in the e-commerce order management process and explains how they are affected by the current business problem.

## 2. Identified Stakeholders

### 1. Customers
**Impact: High**

- Delayed order delivery.
- Difficulty tracking order status.
- Poor customer experience.
- Reduced trust in the store.

### 2. Customer Service
**Impact: High**

- Increased customer inquiries.
- Higher number of complaints.
- Increased workload.
- Lower customer satisfaction.

### 3. Operations Department
**Impact: High**

- Accumulation of unprocessed orders.
- Increased employee workload.
- Difficulty organizing and preparing orders.
- Delays in handing orders to shipping companies.

### 4. Shipping & Delivery Department
**Impact: Medium to High**

- Delayed shipment collection.
- Disrupted delivery schedules.
- Difficulty meeting delivery deadlines.

### 5. Management
**Impact: High**

- Declining Key Performance Indicators (KPIs).
- Potential customer loss.
- Reduced sales and reputation.
- Need for operational improvements.

## 3. Conclusion
The analysis shows that order processing delays affect multiple stakeholders across the business.

Improving the order management process can help increase operational efficiency, improve customer satisfaction, and support better business performance.
